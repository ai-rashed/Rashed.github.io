ABOUT THE PARK

A Home for Wildlife

Inani National Park is presented as a natural conservation environment where forests and wildlife can coexist.

Forest ecosystems are important because they provide food, water, shelter and breeding areas for numerous animals and plants.

Protecting these habitats requires cooperation, environmental education and responsible use of natural resources.
